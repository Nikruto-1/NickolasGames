NickolasGames

Free party games for friends, teams and parties

Play together on one phone. Choose a game, add your players and enjoy the next round. NickolasGames is available in Ukrainian, English, Swedish, German and Spanish.

  • Who Am I? — ask questions and guess the character on your hidden card.
  • Charades — show a word using gestures while the other players guess.
  • Alias — explain words to your friends without saying the word itself.
  • Truth or Dare — choose a question or a challenge for your group.

After the first online visit, you can keep playing offline. Your game progress is saved on this device.
